Open with a sentence or two that says what the reader will get. This first
paragraph sits right under the heading, so make it count.

## A section heading

Normal paragraphs just go on their own lines with a blank line between them.

Use **bold** for the thing you want someone to catch while skimming, and
*italics* for emphasis inside a sentence.

## Links

An ordinary link looks like this: [the down payment calculator](/).

Linking to another site works the same way:
[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/).

Link to your own pages with a slash and no domain — `/`, `/articles`,
`/privacy`, or `/articles/some-other-article`.

## Lists

- First point
- Second point
- Third point

Numbered when order matters:

1. Do this first
2. Then this
3. Finally this

## Quotes and asides

> Pull out a line worth dwelling on with a > in front of it.

## Images (optional)

Put the file in `public/articles/` then reference it without that folder name:

![Describe the image for screen readers](/articles/your-image.jpg)

For a big image at the very top of the article, don't put it in the body —
set `image:` in the block at the top of this file instead.
